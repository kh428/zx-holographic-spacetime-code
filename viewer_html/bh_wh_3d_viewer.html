HaPPY toy black hole and wormhole (static — no foliation, no gauge fixing)

Rendered with the open-source pyzx 3D viewer (github.com/kh428/pyzx_3d_viewer) — companion demo to arXiv:2601.04467 (QPL 2026). Green = Z spiders, blue dashed = Hadamard edges; boundary legs in the disk plane, free bulk legs rising; the black hole's 5 horizon legs rise at the centre. Pauli webs on wires, R/G/B = X/Z/Y. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Needs internet once (three.js import map).

Toy black hole — the n = 2 code with the central tensor REMOVED; the 5 cut bonds are open horizon legs (rising, centre). Overlay: a minimal web on one horizon leg.

Toy wormhole — two black-hole copies STACKED (upper copy mirrored), horizon legs fused into a vertical throat (H-edge parity composed). Overlay: a Pauli web spanning BOTH asymptotic boundaries — a two-sided correlator, drawn.
